◆ YOUR GUIDE TO THE DUNGEON ◆

🎩

MORDECAI

Your System-Appointed Guide

"Ah, there you are. I'm Mordecai — think of me as your agent, your lifeline, and unfortunately, the only friendly voice you'll hear down here. Let's go over the basics, shall we?"

HOW IT WORKS

  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Walls stop you; a shut door stops you until you force it.
  • Action bar: O opens an adjacent door, F fights the mob on Carl's tile, U uses the selected inventory item.
  • The map only shows what Carl has seen — a one-tile circle of light, no torch yet. Everything past it stays black until you walk it; places you've left go dim.
  • Mobs appear as red squares once spotted (the boss square is ringed in gold). A red ✕ marks one you've killed.
  • Every floor has one Safe Room (a teal tile on the map). Stand on it and press O to step inside — its own little map of rooms: a paid bunk that restores HP & SP, Carl's footlocker, Mordecai's Guide Book, and shopkeepers not yet open for business. Walk to the Exit tile and press U to head back out.
  • The card on the right is whatever Carl is standing on — a mob, a doorway, the stairs, the Safe Room, empty floor — or an inventory item you've selected.
  • Kill a mob and you get its XP plus a Loot Box in your inventory.
  • Fill the XP bar to level up: HP & SP fully restore, Carl gains 3 stat points (banked below GOLD, spent in the Safe Room later), a gold payout, and a free Loot Box. His portrait and title climb with him.
  • HP / SP / XP bars are on the left. HP hits zero and the crawl is over — back to the title, with your last save untouched.

ACTIONS

🚪

Open Door O

Force open a closed door in a tile next to Carl. Doors block movement until opened, then stay open. Press O, or use the Open Door slot on the action bar. Standing on a Safe Room tile, O opens the Safe Room instead.

⚔️

Fight F

Engage the mob sharing Carl's tile. The fight takes over the map panel — there's no running once it starts. Each turn costs your action: Fight (F) swing for 1d6 + ⅓ STR − armour; Defend (D) brace for the next blow (+5 Dodge, damage halved), catch your breath (+25% Max SP), and — if the mob whiffs into your guard — line up a riposte that makes your next Fight hit for +50%; or use an inventory item (select it on the left, press U). Every roll and result prints to the System Log; the mob's card stays in the Encounters panel. Win for XP and a Loot Box; lose and the crawl is over. Off a mob, it does nothing.

✋

Use U

Activate the selected inventory item. Click an item in the inventory to select it, then press U (or the Use slot on the action bar). Every item does something different when used — a potion heals, a key unlocks, a loot box opens. With nothing selected, Use just reminds you to pick something first.

🏠

Safe Room O

One per floor, a teal tile on the map. Press O on it to step inside — a small map of its own, fully lit, nothing trying to kill you. Rooms off the central hall: Carl's bunk (press U to sleep — full HP & SP for a gold fee that scales with the floor), Carl's footlocker (U to stash items so a full bag never loses a Loot Box), Mordecai (U opens the Guide Book), and the Shop, Spell Master and Guild Master — staffed, but not open for business yet. The NPCs pipe up in Chat when Carl walks onto them (press U to hear them again); U also works the bunk, footlocker and Mordecai. Walk to the Exit and press O (it's a door) to return to the dungeon where you came in.

CHARACTERS

🧗

Carl

The crawler you're building. A former grunt caught topside when the System swallowed the planet, now clawing his way down floor by floor — barefoot, underslept, and stubborn enough to keep going.

👑

Princess Donut

Carl's cat and combat partner. A pampered, award-winning Persian with a sharp tongue and sharper claws, she pulls more than her weight in a fight and never lets Carl forget it.

🎩

Mordecai

Your System-appointed guide. He handles your listings, feeds you advice through the Guide Book, and is the only friendly voice down here — though "friendly" comes with plenty of dry commentary.

INVENTORY

  • Eight slots, down the left side. More open up as Carl levels (later).
  • Click a slot to select it — the item's card shows in the Encounters panel. Click it again to deselect.
  • Press U (or the Use action) on a selected item to use it. Every item does something different — potions heal, keys unlock, loot boxes open. Most aren't wired up yet.
  • Items work during a fight too — select one and press U — but using it spends Carl's turn, and the mob still gets its swing.
  • Every kill drops a Loot Box straight into the bag. If all eight slots are full it's lost for good, so don't let them pile up.

ATTRIBUTES

  • STR (Strength) — melee damage: a hit does 1d6 + ⅓ STR. Will govern carry weight later.
  • DEX (Dexterity) — accuracy (+DEX to hit), how hard Carl is to hit (Dodge = 10 + ½ DEX), and Max SP (20 + 4×DEX).
  • CON (Constitution) — Max HP (50 + 5×CON). The death clock — nothing else matters if this runs out.
  • LUCK — bonus XP on every kill (+LUCK%) and a wider crit range at 15+. Rare-loot odds later. Does not affect level-up rewards.

Combat is d20 + attribute vs. the target's Dodge, then damage dice minus armour. Every crawl starts with 40 points to split across the four — no respec once you're through the archway.

Leveling: XP to reach a level is 50 × level × (level−1) — 100 for L2, 300 for L3, 600 for L4, and so on. Each level-up fully restores HP & SP, grants 3 stat points (shown below GOLD; you'll spend them in the Safe Room), pays 50 × new level gold, and drops one Loot Box.

SP is shown but does nothing yet — it fuels spells, which arrive on Floor 2.
